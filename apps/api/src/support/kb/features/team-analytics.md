## What it is for
Trends rather than today: weekly throughput, SLA compliance by priority, and how long work takes end to end.

## Who can see it
Managers for their department; Administrators for everything.

## The three things people do here
1. Compare this week's throughput with previous weeks.
2. Find which priority is missing its deadlines most often.
3. Watch the cycle-time trend after making a change.

## Common confusions
- "SLA compliance shows a fraction next to it." The number in brackets is how many *breached*, not how many passed.
