## What it is for
Signing in with email and password, or through a demonstration of Parichay-style single sign-on.

## Important
The Parichay screen is a **sandbox mock** for demonstration. It is not connected to the real national SSO and is clearly labelled as such. It exists to show how the integration would look.

## Common confusions
- "Is this the real Parichay?" No, and the system never claims it is.
- "I am refused after several wrong passwords." Sign-in is rate limited.
