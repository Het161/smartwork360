## What it is for
Your department's work as a board — Pending, In progress, Under review, Completed.

## Who can see it
Managers and Administrators.

## The three things people do here
1. Drag a card to the next column. It saves instantly and is written to the audit record.
2. Create a task with **New task** — when you choose a person, their current workload is shown so work can be spread fairly.
3. Open a card to read its full history.

## Common confusions
- "The card sprang back." Tasks move one step at a time and cannot go backwards from Completed — see INVALID_STATUS_TRANSITION.
- "I cannot create a critical task." The department may have no SLA rule for that priority — see SLA_POLICY_MISSING.
