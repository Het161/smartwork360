## What it is for
Managing people: who exists, what role they hold, which department they are in, and who is waiting to be let in.

## Who can see it
Administrators only.

## The three things people do here
1. Approve or reject pending registrations.
2. Assign or change somebody's department.
3. Change a role, or disable an account that has left.

## Common confusions
- "Disabling somebody deleted their tasks." It does not. Their work stays exactly where it is and needs reassigning — see TASK_ASSIGNEE_INACTIVE.
- "Approving is instant." It is, but it is also audited with your name and your reason.
