## What it is for
Printable and exportable summaries for reporting upward.

## Who can see it
Administrators.

## The three things people do here
1. Pick a month.
2. Read the summary — completion, SLA compliance, department comparison.
3. Export.

## Common confusions
- "The numbers differ slightly from the dashboard." The dashboard is live; a report is a snapshot of the month you selected.
