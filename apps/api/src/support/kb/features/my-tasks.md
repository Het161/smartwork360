## What it is for
Every task assigned to you or created by you, filterable by status and priority.

## Who can see it
Employees see their own tasks only. This is enforced in the database query, not by hiding rows.

## The three things people do here
1. Search by reference number (like REV/2026/0042) or title.
2. Open a task to see its full history — every note, every status change, who did what and when.
3. Add a progress update: move the slider, write one line, submit.

## Common confusions
- "I cannot mark my own task complete." Correct. You submit it for review; somebody else approves it. The person who does the work and the person who signs it off are never the same person.
- "Where did my update go?" It is on the task's timeline, and also written permanently to the audit record.
