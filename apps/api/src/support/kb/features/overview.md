## What it is

SMARTWORK 360 is a task and performance management system for Indian government offices. It does four things: it gives every piece of work an owner and a deadline, it measures whether those deadlines are met, it watches for patterns that suggest misuse, and it records every change permanently so nothing can be quietly rewritten.

## Who uses it

There are three roles, and each sees a different set of screens.

- **Employees** see the work assigned to them, add progress to it, and track their own on-time record.
- **Managers** run a department — they assign work, approve or return what is submitted, and watch workload, morale and burnout across their team.
- **Administrators** see the whole organisation, set the deadline rules, manage people and departments, review fraud alerts, and verify the audit chain.

## How to get started

1. Sign in. You land on the dashboard for your role.
2. Take the two-minute guided tour. It offers itself the first time, and the help button in the bottom-right corner can restart it whenever you like.
3. Work through your own screens: an employee opens a task and adds progress, a manager drags a card on the board and clears the review queue, an administrator reads the department comparison and the fraud centre.

Everything is available in English and Hindi — use the EN / हिंदी control in the top bar.

## The ideas worth knowing

- **Deadlines come from rules, not from guesswork.** Each department sets how many hours a task of each priority gets. That rule is what every SLA figure is measured against.
- **The person who does the work never signs it off.** You submit; somebody else approves. This is why you cannot mark your own task complete.
- **Every change is sealed.** Each audit record carries a hash of the record before it, so altering one breaks the chain and the tampering becomes visible.

## Common confusions

- "Which screen should I be on?" Whichever your role's sidebar shows — the navigation only ever lists what you are allowed to use.
- "I finished my task but it is not counted." Finishing is submitting. It counts once your manager approves it.
