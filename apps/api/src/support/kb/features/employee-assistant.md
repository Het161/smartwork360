## What it is for
A question box for your own tasks. "mere pending tasks?", "kitne overdue", "what is due today" all work.

## Who can see it
Every signed-in user, scoped to what they are allowed to see.

## The three things people do here
1. Tap one of the suggested question chips.
2. Type a question in English, Hindi or Hinglish.
3. Follow the links in the answer straight to the task.

## Common confusions
- "Is it making the numbers up?" No. Every number in the reply is read from the database first and then phrased — it is not generated. If the data is not there, it says so.
- This assistant answers about **your work**. For questions about how the system itself works, or to report an error, use Saarthi Support from the help button instead.
