## What it is for
Who on the team is under too much strain, and why.

## Who can see it
Managers for their own department; Administrators organisation-wide. Employees never see each other's scores.

## The three things people do here
1. Read each person's score out of 100 — higher means more strain.
2. Look at the top two contributing factors, which are shown rather than hidden.
3. Follow the suggested action.

## How the score is built
Five signals over 14 days: active workload, overdue count, share of after-hours activity, share of negative-sentiment updates, and update frequency.

## Common confusions
- "It says I am showing an old week." Scores are calculated per week. If nobody has pressed Recompute since the week rolled over, the most recent week is shown and labelled as such. Press **Recompute** for current numbers.
