## What it is for
The health of your department in one screen: workload, overdue count, cycle time, morale, and burnout risk.

## Who can see it
Managers, for their own department only. Administrators see the organisation-wide version.

## The three things people do here
1. Scan the counters for anything red.
2. Look at the morale gauge — a 14-day average of the sentiment in progress notes.
3. Check the burnout list and act on anybody marked High or Critical.

## Common confusions
- "Morale is negative but nobody complained to me." That is rather the point: the gauge reads the tone of what people write on tasks, which is where frustration usually shows up first.
