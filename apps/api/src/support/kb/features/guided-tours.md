## What it is for
A short guided tour of your own screens, different for each role, in English or Hindi.

## How to use it
It offers itself the first time you sign in. After that, the help button in the bottom-right corner has **Restart tour**.

## What makes it different
Some steps will not advance until you actually do the thing — open an alert, verify the chain, move a card. Reading about a button is not the same as pressing it.

## Common confusions
- "It never appeared." It shows once per role. Restart it from the help button.
- "Can I leave?" Always, in one click or by pressing Escape.
