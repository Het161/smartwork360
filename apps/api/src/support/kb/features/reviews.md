## What it is for
The queue of work your team has submitted and is waiting on you.

## Who can see it
Managers and Administrators.

## The three things people do here
1. Open a submission and read the progress history.
2. Approve it, or send it back.
3. Type the note — it is required either way.

## Common confusions
- "Why must I write a note?" Because "rejected" with no reason is not a decision anybody can act on or audit. The note is stored permanently against your name.
- "Can I approve my own work?" No. Maker and checker are always different people, and the rule is enforced on both routes to completion, not just the button.
