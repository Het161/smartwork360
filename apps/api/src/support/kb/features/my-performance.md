## What it is for
How reliably you finish work on time, over time.

## Who can see it
You see your own. Your manager sees it for their department. Employees cannot see each other's.

## The three things people do here
1. Read the on-time percentage and the trend line.
2. Check the badges earned.
3. Compare this month against previous months.

## Common confusions
- "The score dropped and I did nothing wrong." The score is about deadlines met, and a task that breached its SLA counts against it even if the delay was outside your control. Ask your manager to extend the deadline *before* it breaches rather than after.
