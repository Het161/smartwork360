## What it is for
Things that need your attention: approaching deadlines, breaches, and anomalies involving your team.

## Who can see it
Managers for their department; Administrators for everything.

## The three things people do here
1. Filter by severity.
2. Open an alert to see which task or person it concerns.
3. Act on it, then mark it read.

## Common confusions
- "The counter will not clear." Marking individual alerts read is easy to lose track of; the counter can be cleared in bulk.
