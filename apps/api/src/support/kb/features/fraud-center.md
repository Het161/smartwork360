## What it is for
Patterns that suggest misuse: bulk status changes, after-hours spikes, self-approval attempts, and impossibly fast completions.

## Who can see it
Administrators.

## The three things people do here
1. Sort alerts by score.
2. Open one to see the actual evidence — the specific records, and why they were flagged.
3. Mark it reviewed or dismiss it with a note.

## Common confusions
- "Is the AI accusing this person?" No. It is flagging a pattern for a human to judge, and it shows its working. Detection precision is published on the screen so you know how much to trust it.
- "Somebody dismissed an alert by mistake." It can be reopened.
